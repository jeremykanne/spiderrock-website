Recruiting Process

Frequently Asked Questions

How current are job postings?
The website is continually updated. Jobs are posted as soon as they become available and are removed once the position is filled.
How long does the interview process take?
The timeline varies. It depends on the number of pre-qualified candidates, interview scheduling, the number of short-listed candidates and other factors.
How do I apply for an open position?
Apply for any of our openings through Search Your Possibilities, our careers portal.
Will I need to go through the formal process even if I've interviewed with SpiderRock before?
Yes. Every candidate goes through the same process each time they apply, because the required qualifications vary from position to position.
Will SpiderRock contact me when my résumé is received?
We'd like to speak with every candidate, but we'll reach out when your background matches what the hiring manager is looking for.
How do I apply for an internship?
Internships are posted on the careers portal, just like full-time positions. They're available only through our Chicago office.
Does SpiderRock conduct background checks?
Yes. We run background checks on all new hires before their start date. A typical check verifies employment, Social Security number and education, and includes a criminal record check.
Will I have opportunities for training and development after being hired?
Absolutely. Our internal SpiderVerse program supports continuous development through instructor-led courses, online and on-demand learning, licensing sponsorship and internal SpiderTalk sessions.
Do you recruit new college graduates?
Yes. We love talking with rising talent about roles at SpiderRock, and we take part in campus outreach, including student events, college fairs, panel discussions, mock interviews, presentations, mentorship programs and office tours.
