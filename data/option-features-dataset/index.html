Which Characteristics Are Better Predictors of Returns: Options or Stocks?

The truth lies in combining both. Traditional stock data reflects overall market performance; options data offers a forward-looking lens into market sentiment and volatility, through metrics like delta pressure, implied volatility, and order flow. SpiderRock’s clients use both together to identify correlations, spot trends, and predict returns.
