Our Data & Analytics

Three Product Tiers

Real-Time Data

SpiderRock delivers raw and normalized US and European equity, options, and futures data feeds via multicast channels. Our normalized feed provides a reduced bandwidth profile for better manageability and cost effectiveness, and our client-side APIs turn high multicast packet volumes into individual messages at low latencies.

Learn More →

Options Analytics

Our highly accurate and adaptive pricing methods cover all US-listed options and options on futures. By incorporating data from our API environment into our analytics framework, we offer a low-latency analytics feed providing options content including normalized OPRA and CME, NBBO implied volatility quotes, theoretical surface prices, Greeks, and trade prints.

Learn More →

Historical Data

SpiderRock has a suite of historical data products in multiple asset classes with the ability to deliver data via flat files and RESTful API. Our market data and analytics enable data-driven decisions by examining historical theoretical volatility surfaces, implied volatilities, skew slopes, Greeks, and stock, option, and futures price and print information.

Learn More →

Data Delivery

How We Deliver Data

Multicast Feeds

Raw and normalized low-latency multicast feeds covering US equities, options, and futures, plus European venues via Eurex and Euronext. The normalized format reduces bandwidth while preserving the data integrity institutional workflows require.

Exchange feeds & coverage →

Live APIs

API access to live market data and analytics for single stocks, ETFs, options, and futures. SpiderStream over MLink provides WebSocket and REST access to real-time and delayed institutional-grade data.

SpiderStream / MLink API →

Historical Datasets

10+ years of end-of-day and intraday history — options pricing, Greeks, implied volatility, and equity prices — delivered as Parquet files on AWS S3 or queried in place through Snowflake for backtesting and research.
