Historical Data

Historical Options Price Data

SpiderRock provides historical data feeds that include quotes, volume, and market sizes for each traded asset, along with additional options analytics (implied volatility, Greeks, volatility surfaces, and skews) that help clients better value assets, calculate risk, hedge assets, and identify trends. Historical price feeds consist of archived records of the day’s trading, delivered overnight, in four general formats:

Option Close Marks

EOD

Created immediately after the market closes and when exchanges publish official marks. Contains closing quotes, prices, and markup details for all outright options, using algorithms that estimate the correct theoretical price.

Options Quote Intervals

5 Min

Created every 5 minutes while options markets are open. Contains call and put prices and sizes, the underlying stock price, and SpiderRock fitted surface implied volatilities and Greeks.

Options ATM Volatility Minute Bars

1 Min

Created at 1-minute intervals for all at-the-money options markets by symbol expiration. Includes open/high/low/close values and volatility data.

Options Print Set

Every Print

Created on every option print along with the quote, surface, size, and trade volume at print time, plus all relevant options analytics, exchange-published print types, and SpiderRock’s estimated trade side (buy/sell). Records also contain T+1M and T+10M forward mark details to support trade cost analysis (TCA).

Data quality

All datasets are well-documented with an extensive data dictionary. Data is point-in-time. SpiderRock evaluates the data for validity and accuracy and performs statistical checks on the production databases daily to verify completeness, and includes multiple error codes and surface quality parameters to identify when input data does not support accurate options analytic calculations.

Greeks

Historical Greeks Data

Our historical data packages contain all the option Greeks, including:

Delta

Measures the rate of change of option price with respect to a 1-point change in the underlying price.

Gamma

Measures the rate of change of Delta with respect to a 1-point change in the underlying price.

Vega

Measures the rate of change of the option price with respect to a 1% change in volatility.

Theta

Measures the rate of change of the option price with respect to 1-day change in time to expiration.

Rho

Measures the rate of change of the option price with respect to a 1% change in the interest rate.

Phi

Measures the rate of change of the option price with respect to a 1% change in the dividend rate.

Historical Implied Volatility Calculations

SpiderRock historical implied volatility data uses a family of proprietary pricing models to compute prices, implied volatilities, common options Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho, phi, volga, and vanna), and various scenario risk slides for equity and futures options. Full methodology and technical notes are available on our documentation site.

Historical Data

Historical Stock Price Data

Our historical stock datasets recap the day’s trading activity in the underlying security. Files show open/high/low/close/volume data for stocks, ETFs, and indexes from US exchanges. SpiderRock offers high-quality data that is a point-in-time record of the market, along with corporate action information for historical adjustments in backtesting.

SpiderRock provides four basic formats for stock feed subscriptions:

Stock Close Marks

EOD

Created immediately after the market close when exchanges publish official marks. Contains closing quotes and prices, prior day opening and closing data with corporate action adjustments and daily returns, tracked with reference data and security IDs.

Stock Minute Bars

1 Min

Created once per minute for each open stock and index market. Includes high/low values, spread data, print information, and bid/ask data.

Stock Print Set

Every Trade

Created for each trade with trade cost analysis (TCA). Records are updated 1-minute and 10-minutes after the print.

Stock Imbalances

Every Tick

Buy and sell imbalance records during auctions throughout the trading day, available for both NYSE and ARCA listed securities.

Our stock historical datasets go back 10+ years and support all listed US stocks, ETFs, and ADRs.

Why SpiderRock

Why Use SpiderRock’s Historical Data

Our historical data is derived from the live market data and analytics which power the SpiderRock trading system, ensuring a high level of accuracy and consistency. Datasets are synchronized across asset classes (stocks, options, futures), improving the accuracy of trade performance calculations and supporting multi-asset strategy testing.

Print Sets are typically used to replay market patterns, as they contain every print along with the quote and mid-market surface at print time, with most datasets offering records dating back to 2016.

Trade Cost Analysis

Trade Cost Analysis (TCA)

Market participants can use our TCA metrics data to analyze trade performance. SpiderRock specializes in computing TCA for options and provides similar TCA data for other asset classes as well. Our TCA metrics consider the mean-reverting characteristics of markets and help you understand the true cost of accessing the liquidity.
