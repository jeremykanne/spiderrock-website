Complete Options History

The Full Options Record

SpiderRock's historical options data spans years of end-of-day and intraday tick data across the entire U.S. listed options universe. Each record captures not just prices and volume but computed Greeks and implied volatility derived from SpiderRock's proprietary pricing models.

Whether you're building backtests, training ML models, or conducting academic research, this dataset gives you the depth and completeness to work with confidence.

Data Coverage

What's Included in Every Record

Price & Volume

  • Open, High, Low, Close (OHLC)
  • Volume and open interest
  • Bid/ask and bid/ask sizes
  • Last trade price and size
  • Underlying stock price

Greeks & Risk

  • Delta (direction risk)
  • Gamma (delta sensitivity)
  • Vega (volatility sensitivity)
  • Theta (time decay)
  • Rho (interest rate sensitivity)

Volatility & Meta

  • Implied volatility (bid, ask, mid)
  • Surface volatility from SR model
  • Expiration date and DTE
  • Strike, option type (call/put)
  • Exchange, OCC symbology
