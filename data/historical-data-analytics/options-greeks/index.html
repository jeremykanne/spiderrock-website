The Full Options Record

SpiderRock's historical options data spans years of end-of-day and intraday tick data across the entire U.S. listed options universe. Each record captures not just prices and volume but computed Greeks and implied volatility derived from SpiderRock's proprietary pricing models.

Whether you're building backtests, training ML models, or conducting academic research, this dataset gives you the depth and completeness to work with confidence.

Data Coverage

What's Included in Every Record

Price & Volume

  • Open, High, Low, Close (OHLC)
  • Volume and open interest
  • Bid/ask and bid/ask sizes
  • Last trade price and size
  • Underlying stock price

Greeks & Risk

  • Delta (direction risk)
  • Gamma (delta sensitivity)
  • Vega (volatility sensitivity)
  • Theta (time decay)
  • Rho (interest rate sensitivity)

Volatility & Meta

  • Implied volatility (bid, ask, mid)
  • Surface volatility from SR model
  • Expiration date and DTE
  • Strike, option type (call/put)
  • Exchange, OCC symbology

Use Cases

Who Uses This Data

Quantitative Researchers

Build and backtest options strategies with historically accurate pricing and Greeks. Validate pricing models against actual market data across multiple market regimes.

Machine Learning Teams

Train predictive models on rich options features including Greeks, surface parameters, and volatility regime data across the full listed options universe.

Hedge Funds & Prop Desks

Analyze historical volatility patterns, skew dynamics, and term structure across market cycles to develop edge in options market making and trading.

Academic Researchers

Study options market microstructure, volatility risk premium, and information content of derivatives markets with comprehensive, clean historical data.

Data Delivery

Flexible Access Methods

Historical options and Greeks data is available through multiple delivery channels to fit your workflow — from direct API access to bulk flat file downloads.

  • Liberator API — Programmatic access for targeted queries across dates, underlyings, and expirations
  • Cloud Files — Daily flat files delivered to AWS S3 or Azure Blob Storage for bulk processing
  • Data Portal — Web-based exploration and ad-hoc download for research and validation
