SpiderRock and QuantHouse have been chosen as 2020 Benzinga Listmakers in the category of “Best New Product”. Our product has been selected as an innovative solution that takes a lead on previously unresolved issues and addresses tomorrow’s problems.

QuantHouse and SpiderRock have partnered to add SpiderRock’s proprietary data & analytics service, including implied volatility and option prints, to the 150+ data sources available through QuantHouse’s API. This partnership is the first application of QuantHouse’s API to deliver datasets beyond outright market data and allows SpiderRock to leverage QuantHouse’s deep expertise in market data distribution. As a result, all technology providers that are part of QuantHouse’s API Ecosystem are now also fully integrated with SpiderRock.

The demonstrable advantage for end clients is the ability to consume implied volatilities and options analytics through the same API as global market data, without additional development work. This allows client dev teams to reallocate many hours towards optimization of their own trading models instead of expending them on the labor-intensive process of computing analytics in-house, letting clients to reduce overhead costs without compromising data quality.

The Benzinga Fintech Awards are an annual competition which recognizes companies and individuals who are building the future of financial services and capital markets through leading advancements in technology and innovation. The [Benzinga Fintech Listmakers](https://www.benzinga.com/events/fintech-awards/listmakers/) is an index of carefully-vetted companies and executives within a selection of Fintech categories that are striving to revolutionize the industry.
