We were pleased to see SpiderRock mentioned in Nikhil Adithyan’s recent article, [How to Price Multi-Leg Options Basket with Python](https://medium.datadriveninvestor.com/how-to-price-multi-leg-options-basket-with-python-3464700d8a9a), on DataDrivenInvestor. 

In the article, he showcases how our [Option Basket Calculator](/data/options-pricing-tool/) simplifies the process of pricing complex, multi-leg strategies. Via the SpiderRock [MLink API](/data/spiderstream-market-data-api/), users can define a basket of up to 1,000 option keys and update pricing programmatically throughout the trading day based on current market activity. The API returns leg-level valuations with Greeks, enabling you to treat the basket as a single entity for analysis. 

This makes it easier for traders and quants to explore spreads, collars, and other multi-leg strategies without building pricing logic from scratch.
