The [#volatility](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=volatility&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6655538181070471168) of the stock market as the result of the [#covid19](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=covid19&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6655538181070471168) outbreak continues to subside. Trading costs in SPY ETF options appear to have recovered to near pre-outbreak levels. This recovery might indicate trader confidence in improved market stability although an elevated VIX shows a significant premium for covering equity market risk still remains.

The chart shows [SpiderRock](https://www.linkedin.com/company/spiderrock/)‘s view of the per contract trade cost for SPY options in the three front months for strikes between 25-75 Delta and trades of 100 contracts or less. To learn more: [https://bit.ly/2JeVgn7](https://bit.ly/2JeVgn7)
