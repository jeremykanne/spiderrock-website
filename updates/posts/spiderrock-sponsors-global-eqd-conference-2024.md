SpiderRock is thrilled to announce its sponsorship of the ninth annual [Global EQD conference](https://eqderivatives.com/events/global-eqd-2024) in Las Vegas on May 22-23 at The Wynn.  

This prestigious event will bring together top derivatives practitioners and cross-asset quantitative peers for insightful discussions on strategic positioning in today’s market environment. Attendees can look forward to exploring innovations in portfolio construction, tail risk investing, relative value trading, and portfolio diversification.  

Visit our booth to meet our team and discover how our platform, algorithms, and real-time/historical analytics can optimize your derivatives trading strategies. We look forward to seeing you at the conference.
