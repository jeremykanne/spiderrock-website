SpiderRock is sponsoring the 41st Annual Options Industry Conference in Nashville, Tennessee taking place from April 26th through the 28th. This event enables participants to network with top options professionals and discuss industry related topics such as regulatory changes as well as product and market structure developments. We are happy to announce that Annabelle Baldwin, SpiderRock Partner and CRO will be moderating the session World of Options: International Interest in Options Markets, taking place on Thursday, April 27th, from 9:45 a.m. – 10:30 a.m. CT.

For more information on the conference, please visit OIC’s website: [https://www.optionsconference.com/](https://www.optionsconference.com/). if you would like to register, you can use this link: [http://bit.ly/2rpyqnM](http://bit.ly/2rpyqnM).

We look forward to seeing you in Nashville!
