**Options Data Needs & Considerations Options data is hard.**

Making decisions on investment priorities that involve options is based on a bedrock of solid analytics driven by market data and powerful, low-latency algorithms. Good options market data product can make the difference between strong, profitable – strategies and the rest of the pack. Key questions include:

- What problems does your options data need to solve?
- Is raw data from the exchange enough or do you require deeper options insights?
- What analytics do you require and how does your provider calculate them?
