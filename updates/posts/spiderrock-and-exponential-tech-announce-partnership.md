SpiderRock Gateway Technologies is excited to announce a strategic partnership with [Exponential Tech](https://www.exponential-tech.ai/), a leader in data technology and bespoke signals, data analytics, and global macro forecasts for institutional investors. This collaboration leverages SpiderRock’s robust market data to empower Exponential in creating innovative market behavior analytics and unique market activity monitors for quant researchers and institutional traders.  Through this alliance, Exponential is creating an ever-expanding collection of bespoke signals and analytics based on SpiderRock’s leading derivative analytics and market feeds. 

By combining SpiderRock’s inventory of robust end-of-day (EOD) and intraday option market data and analytics with Exponential’s cutting-edge analytics and research capabilities, this partnership offers traders new and powerful tools to drive decision-making and uncover new insights.  

### **About Exponential Tech:**

Exponential develops bespoke data analytics, signals and global macro forecasts for institutional investors and their team has over 25 years of experience in institutional investment and high-frequency market-making space. Exponential data technology features Unifier TM high-performance hybrid data warehouse and API. Unifier empowers enterprises to innovate more rapidly by leveraging on-prem and on-cloud data technology in combination with tightly integrated Signals and generative-AI-powered analytics. By integrating data into a high-performance and flexible data backplane, Exponential helps businesses of all sizes overcome data entropy and unlock their true analytical capacity as an organization.

### **About SpiderRock:**

SpiderRock Gateway Technologies (“SpiderRock Data & Analytics”) is a division of SpiderRock Technology Solutions, a provider of industry-leading options trading solutions. SpiderRock Gateway is an exchange-licensed redistributor of market data, providing US stocks, options, and futures market data in a raw and normalized format.  

SpiderRock’s proprietary live analytics offer low-cost delivery of market data and options analytics without requiring clients to make a significant investment in infrastructure. In addition, SpiderRock’s robust historical datasets updated daily from live markets are ideal for research, back testing, and making data-driven decisions. 

### **Conclusion:**

This partnership between SpiderRock and Exponential Tech marks a significant step forward in the realm of data-driven decision-making, providing institutional investors with the tools they need to stay ahead in a rapidly evolving market landscape.
