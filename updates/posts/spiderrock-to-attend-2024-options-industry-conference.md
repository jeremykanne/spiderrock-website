SpiderRock will be attending the 42nd annual [Options Industry Conference](https://www.optionsconference.com/), taking place from April 30 to May 2, 2024, at the Omni Grove Park Inn in Asheville, NC. Hosted by OCC and the U.S. option exchanges, this conference stands as the premier event for top-level management and trading professionals in the options industry.

The Options Industry Conference promises an insightful exploration of key topics shaping the options landscape, from regulatory shifts in the U.S. and Europe to the transformative technological developments driving monumental changes in global markets.

At SpiderRock, we recognize the importance of staying at the forefront of industry trends and fostering meaningful connections within the community. This three-day event offers an unparalleled opportunity to engage with a diverse group of industry professionals, influential speakers, and top-quality content.

We look forward to connecting with fellow attendees, diving deep into new topics, and sharing our expertise to contribute to the collective advancement of the options industry. Stay tuned for updates from SpiderRock as we embark on this enriching experience at the Options Industry Conference.
