**We are proud to be a sponsor again of the Global EQD 2021.**

**A Virtual Conference
Day I | May 26, 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM PDT
Day II | May 27, 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM PDT**

Global EQD’s two-day virtual program brings together top asset allocators and portfolio managers to swap strategy insights, network and get updates on everything from tail-risk hedging and trading to alternative risk premia, multi-asset strategies and environmental, sustainable and governance-focused investing.

Strategy discussions will dig into volatility trading in fragile markets, optimizing hedging strategies, the rotation from growth to value and sourcing alpha across asset classes. Institutional investors will debate trends and strategies while leading academics and strategists will present the latest research on overlays, hedging and other quantitative
investing strategies and initiatives.

Visit our virtual table to learn more about our trading platform, risk management solutions as well as data & analytics products.
