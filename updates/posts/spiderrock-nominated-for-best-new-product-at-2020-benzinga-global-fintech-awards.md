SpiderRock is pleased to announce that we, in partnership with QuantHouse, have been nominated for [“Best New Product”](https://bit.ly/32Y0rml) at the [2020 Benzinga Global Fintech Awards](https://www.benzinga.com/events/fintech-awards/). Our nomination is for SpiderRock’s proprietary data and analytics feed that is now available at 20 datacenters worldwide via the global QuantLINK network. Our data and analytics feed includes implied volaltility, Greeks, risk slides, and high-quality volatility surfaces across all US-listed equity and futures options, integrated in real-time with key elements of raw exchange feeds.

Voting is now open! Please take a moment to vote for our application here: [https://bit.ly/32Y0rml](https://bit.ly/32Y0rml)

The [Benzinga Fintech Listmakers](https://www.benzinga.com/events/fintech-awards/listmakers/) is an index of carefully-vetted companies and executives within a selection of Fintech categories that are striving to revolutionize the industry. Winners will be announced on November 10, 2020 at the virtual event celebrating innovation and excellence in Fintech.
