In the last installment of our five-part series on finding the right data partner we explored [provider service models and stability](/updates/evaluating-your-options-data-partner-part-3-provider-service-model-and-stability/). Now, let’s delve into another crucial aspect: the total cost of ownership (TCO). Understanding TCO is vital when considering the complexities and costs associated with obtaining, managing, and supporting an options data infrastructure.

**The Complexity and Cost of Options Data Infrastructure**

Options data infrastructure is not just a simple matter of data access; it’s an intricate ecosystem that requires substantial investment in both technology and human resources. The costs involved are multifaceted.

For instance, exchange fees vary depending on whether you need end-of-day, delayed, or real-time data, and each type of data access incurs different costs, requiring accurate budgeting. Additionally, connectivity fees are influenced by your existing systems’ capabilities and the specific requirements of options data delivery. Upgrading or adapting your infrastructure to meet these needs can be expensive.

## **Evaluating Capabilities and Costs**

However, beyond these direct costs, the real challenge lies in evaluating the comprehensive capabilities a partner offers and how these contribute to the total cost.

 Handling options data effectively requires a highly educated and specialized team. Building this expertise in-house involves significant expenses in hiring, training, and retaining talent. Moreover, modeling calculations and warehousing large data sets require robust hardware and infrastructure, leading to substantial capital expenditure. Many trading organizations struggle to excel in all aspects of options data management when relying solely on in-house resources. Similarly, many options data providers focus narrowly on data or technology without offering comprehensive modeling capabilities.

Achieving success in the options market requires excellence across the entire data stack, which can be challenging and costly to achieve independently.

## **The Benefits of Outsourcing to Specialized Partners**

Outsourcing these costs to a specialized partner’s platform offers significant advantages. By leveraging a partner’s existing infrastructure and expertise, organizations can materially drive down costs. This includes savings on hardware, software, and human resources.

Specialized partners bring in-depth quantitative expertise, which is crucial for effective data modeling and analysis. This not only saves costs but also enhances the quality of insights and decisions. Furthermore, a partner that goes beyond providing raw data to offer data wrangling and quantitative modeling can dramatically reduce processing time. This efficiency translates into a higher return on investment, leveraging both skill and scale.
