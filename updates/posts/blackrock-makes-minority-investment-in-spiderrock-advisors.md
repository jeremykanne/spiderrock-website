We are extremely excited and proud this morning to announce that BlackRock, Inc (through a subsidiary) has made a minority investment into our affiliate – SpiderRock Advisors. The investment shows confidence of a major industry leader in the SpiderRock Advisors business model and team and the importance and utility of SpiderRock Platform’s industry leading technology that underpins it. Congratulations to the SpiderRock Advisors team. The press release from BlackRock can be found below.
