SpiderRock explores how generative AI can be applied to automate the discovery of predictive features in US equity markets, combining options market signals with machine learning techniques to surface non-obvious patterns in price behavior and volatility.

Traditional quantitative research requires domain expertise to identify which combinations of market signals are predictive. Generative AI changes this dynamic — by systematically iterating over large feature spaces derived from options analytics, including implied volatility surfaces, Greeks, skew metrics, and order flow proxies, researchers can surface candidate features that would otherwise take months to identify manually.

SpiderRock's rich options data fabric provides the raw material for this kind of research: live and historical implied volatility, normalized Greeks, term structure analytics, and microstructure signals across all US listed equities and ETFs. When fed into generative AI workflows, these datasets enable a new class of systematic feature discovery that complements traditional quantitative research.
