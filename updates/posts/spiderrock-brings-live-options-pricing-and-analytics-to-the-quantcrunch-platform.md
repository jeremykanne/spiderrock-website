CHICAGO, July 14, 2026 — SpiderRock, a provider of institutional level options and analytics, historical data, and trading technology solutions, today announced a partnership with QuantCrunch, a derivatives analytics platform specializing in options pricing and analytics across equity and commodity markets.

The partnership integrates SpiderRock's live options analytics into the QuantCrunch platform, providing users with access to institutional-grade theoretical pricing, volatility analytics, and market intelligence within a unified derivatives environment.

By combining SpiderRock's analytics with QuantCrunch's proprietary tools for market analysis, strategy back testing, structure evaluation, and content creation, clients can streamline research and decision-making workflows across trading, sales, and quantitative investment strategy (QIS) teams.

### About SpiderRock

SpiderRock Data & Analytics is a division of SpiderRock Technology Solutions, a provider of industry-leading options trading solutions. SpiderRock Data & Analytics is an exchange-licensed redistributor of market data, providing US and EU stocks and options market data in a raw and normalized format.

SpiderRock's proprietary live analytics offer a low-cost delivery of market data and options analytics without requiring clients to make a significant investment in infrastructure. In addition, SpiderRock's robust historical datasets updated daily from live markets are ideal for research, back testing, and making data-driven decisions.

### About QuantCrunch

QuantCrunch is a derivatives analytics platform that helps financial professionals analyze, visualize, and manage complex options and derivatives markets. The platform combines proprietary analytics, strategy back testing, risk management tools, real-time market analysis, and customizable reporting within a single workflow, enabling trading, sales, and investment teams to transform market data into actionable insights.
