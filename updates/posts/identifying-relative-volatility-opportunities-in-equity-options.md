### **Understanding Relative Volatility Opportunities**

Pairs trading is a relative value strategy that capitalizes on the mispricing between two correlated securities. SpiderRock’s chart tool is a game-changer in this area and empowers traders to evaluate opportunities not only for underlying stock pairs but also for volatility pairs. 

The video introduces us to a volatility pair involving Microsoft (MSFT) and Apple (AAPL) and showcases the capabilities of SpiderRock’s chart tool. The chart is divided into three panels, each providing key insights to guide your trading decisions.
