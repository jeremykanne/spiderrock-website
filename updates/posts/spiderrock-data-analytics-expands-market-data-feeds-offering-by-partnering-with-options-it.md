SpiderRock Gateway Technologies and Options IT have partnered to add the 100+ low-latency feeds from Options IT through [SpiderRock’s API](/data/spiderstream-market-data-api/). SpiderRock and Options IT are both well-recognized data vendors within the industry. This partnership represents an innovative application of existing technology from both sides, enabling end users to receive market data feeds through a single market-leading API.

SpiderRock is a licensed distributor of [raw and normalized market data products](/data/), currently providing over 30 feeds focusing on the US consolidated equity feeds, options, futures, auctions, and complex order book feeds. SpiderRock is also well known within the US options sector for producing exclusive analytics and offering the most accurate [volatility surfaces](/data/historical-data-analytics/volatility-surfaces/) available on the market.

Options IT is a leading Capital Markets service provider of high-quality raw and normalized data providing clients with consolidated access to global market data sources across multiple asset classes. Adopting the Options IT feeds as a standalone product through SpiderRock’s API noticeably improves SpiderRock’s data offering and leverages the deep expertise to meet the client’s needs.

For more information on how clients can benefit from this partnership, please [contact us.](mailto:datasales@spiderrock.net)

**About Options IT:**

Options Technology is the No. 1 provider of IT infrastructure and market data to global Capital Markets firms, supporting their operations and ecosystems. Our Low Latency Raw Market Data covers over 300+ Global Exchanges, Equities, Futures, Options, Fixed Income, and FX in Exchange native format.

Founded in 1993, the company provides high-performance managed trading infrastructure and market data to over 550 firms globally. Options clients include the leading global investment banks, hedge funds, funds of funds, proprietary trading firms, market makers, broker/dealers, private equity houses, and exchanges. With offices in 9 key cities; New York, Toronto, Chicago, London, Belfast, Hong Kong, Singapore, Tokyo, and Auckland, Options are well placed to service its customers on-site and remotely.
