In the world of financial markets, access to real-time data is crucial for making informed decisions. For many, solutions like Bloomberg’s B-PIPE or BVOL offer a comprehensive solution, but at a high price point. SpiderRock Data & Analytics’ [**SpiderStream over MLink Market Data API**](/data/spiderstream-market-data-api/) offers a powerful, cost-effective alternative for accessing live U.S. equities, futures, and options market data and analytics. 

Built with developers in mind, SpiderStream over MLink provides a flexible, modern API for streaming and querying live data. Its internet-based delivery simplifies the onboarding process, removing the hurdles often tied to legacy providers like B-PIPE or BVOL. Whether you’re building trading tools, analytics dashboards, or enterprise platforms, SpiderStream delivers the performance and coverage you need without the added complexity or cost.

### **Key Features of MLink:**

- **Live Data Streaming and Querying:** Stream or query real-time and delayed data feeds across six product categories, including stocks, futures, options, market summaries, imbalances, analytics, and more.
- **Flexible Message Delivery:** Choose from JSON, Framed JSON, or Protobuf protocols to match your application design and performance needs.
- **WebSocket and REST Connections:** Use WebSocket for live subscriptions to data or REST for occasional queries. MLink supports both options to fit your use case.
- **Real-Time and Delayed Data:** Developers can access both real-time and delayed data, ensuring the most up-to-date information for their applications.

Backed by SpiderRock Data & Analytics’ infrastructure and security standards, [**SpiderStream over MLink Market Data API**](/data/spiderstream-market-data-api/) is a scalable solution designed for reliability and ease of use. It provides the benefits of a premium data feed, with pricing and flexibility better aligned to the needs of modern financial firms. 

If you are evaluating alternatives to Bloomberg BVOL or B-PIPE for volatility or pricing data, MLink deserves a close look.
